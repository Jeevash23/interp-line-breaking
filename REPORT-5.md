T overall: pass


- **T passes.**
  - The leftover token weight is 0.162 at width 20, 0.099 at 30 and 0.050 at 40. At widths 60–120 it is within ±0.04 of zero. In code it is 0.036–0.061 and in German −0.004 to 0.019.
  - Across the 11 conditions it falls with the mean number of tokens on the line (rank correlation −0.91). So the width-40 residual of rounds 1–3 is the visible end of a bias set by how few tokens a line holds, not by the width as such.
  - Without L5H7 the bias is 0.60 at width 20 and 0.23 at width 120 (T3). L5H6's attention to the line start falls by 0.0146 per extra token at width 20 and by 0.0038 at width 80, with characters used held fixed (T4): dilution is steepest on short lines.
  - L5H7 corrects in every condition, by 0.23–0.56, code and German included (T5). It removes 73% of the bias at width 20 and 91% or more at widths 40–120 (slightly over 100% at widths 100 and 120, where the intact weight is just below zero). On this text the intact interval includes 0 at widths 60, 100 and 120, and its lower bound is 0.000 at width 80.
- Not predicted: at width 120 the per-token attention slope of L5H6 is steep again (−0.0130), as in exploration. The dilution account covers widths 20–100.
- Not as the account stated: on this text, code keeps slightly more leftover weight than English at the same widths (0.061 and 0.036, against 0.050 and 0.015). On exploration text it kept less (0.045 and 0.041, against 0.098 and 0.057). Its bias without L5H7 is about half of English's, and L5H7 removes a smaller share of it (80% and 87%). German keeps less than English, as stated. No frozen part tested this comparison; T2's rank correlation, which includes code, passed.

- **M passes.**
  - Freezing MLPs 21 and 23 at the decision token makes the count's effect 41% larger and the width's 54% larger. With the final LayerNorm's statistics frozen as well, the figures are 39% and 53% (M1). The damping is a direct write to the logits, not the LayerNorm route of entropy neurons.
  - Freezing only MLP23.736 and MLP21.1410 reproduces 98% and 96% of it (M2).
  - On natural text, ablating the two neurons raises the newline's log-probability by 0.38 where the newline is the likely next token. For other likely tokens the change is −0.05, and their entropy barely moves (M3). Ablating the whole MLPs instead lowers other tokens by 0.43.
  - The two neurons' activations correlate 0.83 and 0.78 with the newline log-odds at decision points (M4). They fire as a break becomes likely and push back against it: negative feedback on the newline alone.
- The damping is larger on this text than on the round-4 text (round 4: 36% and 44%).

- **S passes.**
  - The round-4 circuit alone reproduces the decision's size no better than a constant (fidelity −0.16) while keeping the ranking (AUROC 0.949).
  - Adding the 24 components from the fidelity search raises fidelity to 0.785, the separation kept to 0.65 and the width effect kept to 0.71 (S1–S3). The same number of random components does almost nothing (fidelity at most 0.12, separation at most 0.36).

- **Stated plainly:** the fuller circuit is about four-fifths sufficient under mean ablation and about one-fifth to one-third under resample ablation. The 24 added components were chosen to restore fidelity, not because the account explains their role; most are early heads that attend to earlier lines, plus MLPs 9, 13 and 19.

Their verdicts (C holds on one item; S and R do not hold, with intervals spanning tens of units) carry almost no information. Every other cell has 88–100 items. A width-20 test of the count and width roles needs a larger held-out pool.

## Limits
- One model, with greedy wrapping, on one more English batch and two new text types. The code is reflowed standard-library source, not code as written.
- Thresholds were set at about half the explored sizes, with no correction across the 16 parts.
- The fuller circuit was chosen by a greedy search on train and named only by its parts. Its held-out values show that the choice generalises, not what the added parts do.
- The damping neurons were found by gradient attribution on train and tested causally here. Smaller contributions from other neurons are not ruled out.
- The width-20 count and width items are too few to read (above).
