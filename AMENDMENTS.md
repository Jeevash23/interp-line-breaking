# D1 charter amendments

Changes to `CHARTER.md` after its freeze, dated, never silent edits. Each amendment's digest is in `FROZEN.md`.

## Amendment 1 (6 Nov 2026, evening): round-4 exploration

**Why.** After three confirmation rounds the author asked for more digging before any write-up (the author's go-ahead, quoted in the original record). The charter's question is unchanged. This amendment adds five exploration items, one model use, one download and one new held-out text batch. Everything done under it is **exploratory** until a frozen `PROTOCOL-4.md` tests it on held-out text.

**Items, in order of priority.**
1. **The decision step.** Where does Pythia-410M combine the running count (written at the decision token by layer 5) with the stored width (read by L8H15, L17H1 and L6H2) and turn them into the newline logit? Candidates from exploration that were never tested causally: L17H1's direct write, MLPs 14, 16 and 18, and MLP23, whose ablation raised the separation (exp04). Tools: path patching and mediation from the readers and counters to later components, direct-logit attribution, ablation of candidate MLPs and heads at the decision token, with random-component controls of matched size and layer.
2. **Sufficiency.** Keep only the found circuit (counters L2H7, L4H15, L5H6 and L5H7; correctors L5H7 and L4H14; the newline writers; readers L8H15, L17H1 and L6H2; and whatever item 1 finds) and ablate everything else, by mean and by resample ablation. Measure how much of the break/no-break separation and of the width effect remains. Sufficiency will be measured layer-wise and position-wise as needed (for example, keeping all components at positions other than the decision token), because ablating every other component at every position may remove things no account claims to explain, such as next-token prediction itself.
3. **Geometry**, against the Claude 3.5 Haiku account (Gurnee et al., arXiv 2601.04480: curved low-dimensional count manifolds, boundary heads rotating them). Is Pythia's count curved and low-dimensional, with reader heads rotating it towards the width, or is it closer to a scalar, such as L5H6's attention-dilution share? Tools: PCA and curvature of the class means of the residual and of head outputs by characters used, as in the Haiku paper; the readers' query–key products as a function of the count and the previous line's length. Saved activations (`outputs/exp03_*`) are used where they fit.
4. **Why GPT-2 Small fails.** For each role (counters, correctors, stored width, readers, decision step), search GPT-2 Small (on disk) for components that play it, with the same tests used in Pythia-410M and D1-X. Which roles are missing or weak?
5. **Training dynamics.** When do the counters, the correctors, the stored width and the readers appear during Pythia-410M's training? Up to six intermediate checkpoints, about 0.9 GB each (about 5.4 GB in total), approved by the author ("0.9 gb is fine"), for example steps 1k, 4k, 16k, 32k, 64k and 143k (the final model is on disk). More than six is parked for the author. This item is descriptive and may stay exploratory.

**Material.**
- Exploration: the WikiText-2 train split and constructed inputs, as before.
- Confirmation: WikiText-2 has no unread text left, and every part of the first new-text batch is assigned (round 3 or D1-X). A second batch of new Wikipedia text is therefore fetched for round 4 (`code/fetch_newtext2.py`, same selection rule with different starting days, 15 pages per start, no article shared with the first batch). It is split by article hash into `conf4-pythia410m` (round 4's confirmation) and `reserve-pythia410m` (kept unread for a later round). Both parts are frozen as soon as the fetch finishes, before any script reads them, and nothing in exploration reads them.

**Models.** Pythia-410M (final checkpoint and up to six intermediate checkpoints), GPT-2 Small. Pythia-1B, Qwen and OLMo stay with D1-X.

**Budget.** Up to two weeks for items 1–5. Local machine only, in the memory cage, GPU memory under about 5 GB, one job at a time under the shared lock `~/.cache/d1-gpu.lock`, with at most one D1 job waiting on it. Downloads: the second text batch (a few MB) and the six checkpoints (about 5.4 GB, approved). No Modal.

**What counts as a candidate finding.** As in the charter. For item 1: a small set of components at or after layer 6 whose removal at the decision token cuts the count and width effects on the newline clearly more than random sets of the same size and layers, and which predicts a specific effect on held-out text. For item 2: a stated share of separation kept, with random-circuit controls of the same size. Items 3–5 may end in a description rather than a candidate.

**Stop rules.** Stop when items 1–4 each have a candidate or have been searched without one, and item 5 has been described; or at two weeks. Park for the author anything needing more than six checkpoints, Modal, or a new target.

## Amendment 2 (6 Nov 2026, late evening): round-5 robustness exploration

**Why.** An outside review of the paper draft (v1) listed four weaknesses, all of them limits the draft states itself: (a) the circuit is only partly sufficient (30% of the separation, and its early part needs heads outside the account); (b) a residual token-count bias at width 40; (c) the damping by MLPs 21 and 23 is described but not explained; (d) only Wikipedia prose at widths 40, 60 and 80. The author asked to address them (the author's request, quoted in the original record). The charter's question is unchanged. Everything done under this amendment is **exploratory** until a frozen `PROTOCOL-5.md` tests it on held-out text.

**Items.**
1. **Sufficiency.** Find the further components the circuit needs. (i) Label every head in layers 0–5 by its attention role on train text (previous token, current word, line start, first token, other) and add whole role classes to the circuit; (ii) a greedy search that adds, one at a time, the early head (and, separately, the late head or MLP at the decision token) that most raises the separation kept, against random additions of the same number. Report a faithfulness curve (separation, AUROC and width effect kept as components are added) and the smallest set found that reaches a stated share. The aim is a stated sufficiency figure for a fuller circuit, not a forced 100%.
2. **The width-40 token bias.** Explain where the residual token weight at width 40 comes from, not remove it. Candidate: L5H6 counts by dilution, which is non-linear in the number of tokens on the line, while L5H7's correction is closer to linear, so the correction is wrong where lines hold few tokens. Measures: the token weight by tokens on the line and by width, intact and with L5H7 ablated; L5H6's and L5H7's line-start attention and outputs as functions of tokens and characters. The result is turned into a prediction for widths not yet measured and for text with a different number of characters per token.
3. **The damping.** Test whether MLPs 21 and 23 do generic confidence regulation (entropy neurons acting through the final LayerNorm, or token-frequency neurons; Stolfo et al., NeurIPS 2024, arXiv 2406.16254) or something specific to the newline. Measures: the damping with the final LayerNorm scale frozen; the neurons that carry it (direct effect on the newline log-odds, weight norm, share of the output weight in the unembedding's null space, alignment with the newline's unembedding direction); whether the same MLPs damp other confident predictions in natural text.
4. **Generality.** Pythia-410M on (i) widths 20, 30, 100 and 120 of English Wikipedia text; (ii) Python source code, whitespace collapsed and rewrapped (the Python 3.14 standard library already on disk); (iii) German Wikipedia text (new articles created in 2025–26, the same selection rule, a few MB). For each: the behaviour, the counters, the token corrector, the stored width and the readers, measured as in D1-X's battery. Verse and lists are not used: no clean local source exists, and verse breaks lines by metre rather than by width, so greedy wrapping does not apply.

**Material.**
- Exploration: WikiText-2 train, constructed inputs, and the exploration parts of the two new text types.
- New text types: each split by a hash of its source (file path or page id) into an exploration part and a confirmation part (`conf5-code`, `conf5-de`), frozen as soon as they are built or fetched, before any script reads them. The German fetch follows `code/fetch_newtext2.py`'s rule on de.wikipedia.org, one request at a time.
- Confirmation: the unread `reserve-pythia410m` part of the second English batch, and the two `conf5-*` parts. None of them is read in exploration.

**Models.** Pythia-410M only. **Budget:** up to a week; local machine only, in the memory cage, one job at a time under `~/.cache/d1-gpu.lock` with at most one D1 job waiting; downloads: German text only, a few MB; no Modal.

**What counts as a candidate finding.** Item 1: a set whose separation kept clearly exceeds 30% and that of random sets of the same size and layers. Item 2: a variable that accounts for the residual token weight across widths and gives a prediction for new widths or text types. Item 3: evidence for or against the generic account (LayerNorm route, null-space neurons, damping of other confident tokens). Item 4: for each text type and width, which roles hold under the D1-X thresholds and which do not.

**Stop rules.** Stop when each item has a candidate or has been searched without one, or at a week. Park for the author anything needing Modal, a new model or a download over the standing limit.
