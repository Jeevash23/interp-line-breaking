# D1 third confirmation protocol (CONFIRMATORY): new text

Written 6 Nov 2026 at the author's request to address D1's limits ("New text, then models"). This protocol, its four scripts, the text loader, the new-text parts and the fetch script were digested in `FROZEN.md` before any script read the new text for results. Later changes are dated entries in `AMENDMENTS.md`.

## Why a third round
Rounds 1 and 2 used WikiText-2. That is Wikipedia text from before 2017, which Pythia may well have seen in training, inside the Pile. Round 3 repeats the round-2 tests on prose Pythia cannot have seen: Wikipedia articles created between January 2025 and September 2026.

## Material and model
- **Model:** Pythia-410M (on disk), float32, local GPU.
- **Text:** `data/newtext/part-conf-pythia410m.jsonl`.
  - **Source:** English Wikipedia, through the public API, fetched on 6 Nov 2026. The licence is CC BY-SA 4.0. Source, fetch date, selection rule and counts are in `data/newtext/MANIFEST.md`.
  - **Selection rule** (in `code/fetch_newtext.py`):
    - articles from the page-creation log, read forward from the 1st and 15th of each month;
    - creations whose summary mentions a split, merge, copy, move, translation or draft are skipped;
    - pages that are not redirects, with at least 6,000 bytes of wikitext;
    - paragraphs of 600–1,800 characters, Latin script, with no formulae; at most 3 per article.
  - **The split:** articles are divided into 7 parts by a hash of the page id. This part is for Pythia-410M's confirmation; the others are reserved for the cross-model study (`xmodel/CHARTER.md`).
  - **Size:** 264 paragraphs from 146 articles (counted from the part file before the freeze; no text was read for results).
  - **Paragraph order:** seed 100, as in rounds 1 and 2. The text is used as fetched, with no WikiText detokenisation.
- **Samples:** the same rules as round 2 (token regressions: the first 60 paragraphs per width; width items: up to 150 at width 60, random seed 200). Ablation means come from WikiText-2 train, as before.

## Predictions
These are what `REPORT-2.md` supports, with the original thresholds unchanged. The scripts `code/c3_01`…`c3_04` are copies of the frozen `c2_01`…`c2_04`; only the text loader, the allowed split name and the output names change, plus one added verdict line in `c3_01`.

- **T1, token correction by L5H7** (`c3_01_token.py`). Passes if all of these hold:
  - **H1b**, at widths 40, 60 and 80: L5H7 ablated gives a coefficient ≥ 0.30, with lower bound > 0.20.
  - **H1c**, at all three widths: controls L5H3, L5H11 and L5H14 each within 0.10 of intact.
  - **H1a**, at widths 60 and 80: the intact 95% interval lies within (−0.10, +0.10).
  - **Width 40:** the intact model keeps a small token weight, with the lower bound of its 95% interval above 0. Two splits showed this, so it is predicted here rather than H1a.
  
  The original H1, all parts at all widths under the frozen 0.10 limit, is also reported for continuity. It is expected to fail at width 40.
- **R2, width readers** (`c3_02_width.py`). Thresholds as `PROTOCOL.md` H2:
  - positive control: the width-effect interval above 0;
  - L8H15 ≥ 25%;
  - L8H15, L17H1 and L6H2 together ≥ 50%;
  - the mean of 10 random triples < 15%.
- **H3, L4H14 as a second corrector** (`c3_01_token.py`): L4H14 ablated minus intact ≥ 0.10 at each width, with the paired interval above 0.
- **H4, the counting heads re-used at the newline** (`c3_03_newline.py`). Thresholds as `PROTOCOL-2.md`:
  - H4a: every newline, ≥ 25% removed, with lower bound > 0;
  - H4b: the last newline, ≥ 25%, with lower bound > 0;
  - H4c: random sets < 15% on average;
  - H4d: newline-only ablation keeps ≥ 80% of the separation, and all-position ablation keeps < 30%;
  - the positive control.
- **H5, routes** (`c3_04_paths.py`): L8H15 value minus key > 0, and L17H1 key minus value > 0, each with its interval above 0; the positive control.
- **H6, the stored length is token-corrected** (`c3_03_newline.py`):
  - at widths 40 and 60, L5H7 ablated at newlines minus intact ≤ −0.03, with the paired interval below 0;
  - L5H11 at newlines changes the coefficient by less than 0.02.
  
  Width 80 is reported without a verdict.

What counts against each prediction is the same as in `PROTOCOL-2.md`: any part failing at any of the widths named above.

## Analysis
- **Bootstrap and verdicts:** as in round 2, with 2,000 resamples and seed 0, and paired where stated. No multiple-test correction, because the thresholds are fixed in advance. A post hoc Holm analysis of rounds 1 and 2 is kept separately in `POSTHOC.md`.
- **Tables:** generated by `code/report3_tables.py` from the saved outputs and checked verbatim against `REPORT-3.md`.
- **Pipeline checks:** `--smoke` on the WikiText-2 train split before the freeze. Those are not results.

## Stopping rules
- One run per script.
- A crash is fixed with a dated note in `AMENDMENTS.md`.
- No re-runs to improve a result.
- Results are written up as they come out.

## Compute
Local machine, about 10 minutes of GPU time, in the memory cage, one job at a time. No further downloads.

## Order of runs
1. Freeze: digests in `FROZEN.md`; files made read-only.
2. `run.sh c3_01_token.py conf-pythia410m`.
3. `run.sh c3_02_width.py conf-pythia410m`.
4. `run.sh c3_03_newline.py conf-pythia410m`.
5. `run.sh c3_04_paths.py conf-pythia410m`.
