## Tests and thresholds
G, C, T, S and R are as defined in `PROTOCOL-X1.md`. Stored length uses the S form: in exploration the decision kept 79–84% of its separation, a clear dissociation, as in Qwen2.5-0.5B.

Thresholds are about half the smaller of the two explored sizes, rounded down. The decision-kept threshold for S is 15–20 points below the smaller explored value.

| Test | Set | Explored (train / new text) | Threshold | Random limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C | L5H21, L7H22, L9H7 | 79% / 72% | share ≥ 0.35 | mean < 0.15 |
| T | L5H21, L3H2 | ablated 0.23, 0.24, 0.17 / 0.24, 0.27, 0.22 at widths 40, 60, 80 | ≥ 0.08 at 40, 60 and 80 | mean < 0.10 |
| S | L5H21, L9H7, L6H25, L2H17 | 26%, kept 79% / 31%, kept 84% | share ≥ 0.13; kept ≥ 0.60 | mean < 0.15 |
| R | L11H21 | 55% / 54% | share ≥ 0.25 | mean < 0.15 |

## Predictions that would not be made from the earlier models alone
- **One reader head.** A single head (L11H21), out of 32 in its layer, carries at least a quarter of the stored length's effect at the decision.
- **One head in three tested roles.** L5H21 is in C, T and S. Unlike the dilution heads that play these roles in Qwen3 (L4H1) and Gemma (L6H3), its attention to the line-start newline *rises* as the line fills (r = +0.59 in exploration). The second token corrector, L3H2, is a dilution head (r = −0.89).
- **Stored length with a dissociation.** The newline writers carry part of the stored length (share ≥ 0.13), while the decision keeps at least 60% of its separation without them. This differs from Qwen3, where removing the writers also damaged the decision.
